How it works

  1. Prove control of a domain. Add one DNS record. Nothing is scanned before it is in place, and it is checked again before every scan.
  2. Choose how deep to look. Passive reads public records only and sends nothing to your hosts. Probe makes one ordinary request per host. Active checks open ports and known misconfigurations, read-only.
  3. Read the report, then hear about changes. The first report is the map. After that, scans repeat on a schedule and you are told what is new, what was fixed, and what changed: a new signer on a Safe, a certificate about to expire, a lookalike domain that started taking mail.

What it never does

  • It never scans a domain without proof of control, and it never guesses hostnames.
  • It never contacts private addresses, the addresses of other organisations, or a host whose operator asked to be left alone.
  • It never stores a password or a secret. A leaked credential is reported by where it is, not by what it is.
  • It never sends your domain to a third-party lookup service to be matched against a list. Lists are downloaded and matched here.
  • It never shows one organisation anything about another.
